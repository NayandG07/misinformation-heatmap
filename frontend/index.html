Multi-Layer Detection Engine

🧠
Layer 01

Multilingual Regional NLP

Pre-trained transformer architecture parsing headlines and article text in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, and 15+ Indian regional dialects.

🔍
Layer 02

Fact-Check Cross-Reference

Cross-references incoming stories against verified debunked claims from Alt News, Boom Live, Quint WebQoof, and India Today Fact Check.

📡
Layer 03

Real-Time Ingestion Engine

Monitors RSS feeds from TOI, The Hindu, NDTV, Indian Express, and GDELT global event indexes through concurrent asynchronous streaming.

🗺️

Understanding the Platform

Everything you need to know about our AI-powered misinformation detection system.

IndicBERT is an ALBERT-based multilingual model pre-trained on 12 major Indian languages (and fine-tuned for 20+ dialects). It captures unique semantic nuances, script variations, and local idioms that traditional English-only models miss.
Our 5-model ensemble classifier achieves an average accuracy of 91% on benchmark datasets by combining linguistic pattern recognition, source domain reputation, and direct cross-referencing with certified IFCN fact-checking networks.
The ingestion engine polls top Indian RSS feeds every 2 minutes and processes GDELT global news events continuously via Server-Sent Events (SSE), ensuring near-instant updates on the interactive heatmap.
You can visit our dedicated Analytics Dashboard page to explore real-time charts, event breakdowns, accuracy metrics, and system status health logs.
